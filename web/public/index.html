Tasks that stay on your machine

DevTask is a local-first, keyboard-driven task manager for developers. No account required, no server involved — until you decide otherwise.

Download View source

Free and open source · macOS, Windows, Linux

Why it's different

Yours by default

Everything lives in a SQLite file on your disk. No sign-up, no network, no telemetry. Export it whenever you want.

Capture in one keystroke

Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Space from anywhere drops a task in without breaking what you were doing.

Structure without sprawl

Contexts hold groups, groups hold tasks, tasks hold subtasks. Enough to stay organised, not enough to become a second job.

Sync only if you want

Sign in to sync your own tasks across your own machines, or share a workspace with a team. Off unless you turn it on.
